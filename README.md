# KizunaCore (キズナコア)

![KizunaCore Banner](assets/banner.png) <!-- Replace with your actual banner image if available -->

**KizunaCore** is an emotional AI companion app designed to create meaningful connections through interactive chat, virtual gifts, and blockchain-powered transactions. Experience a unique bond with your virtual companion, powered by cutting-edge technology.

---

## Why KizunaCore?

In a world where digital interactions are becoming more common, **KizunaCore** brings warmth and personality to your everyday life. Whether you're looking for companionship, entertainment, or a way to express yourself, KizunaCore offers a one-of-a-kind experience.

- **Emotional Connection**: Chat with a virtual companion who remembers your name, preferences, and important dates.
- **Personalized Gifts**: Show your affection by sending virtual gifts like roses, chocolates, and more.
- **Blockchain Integration**: Use meme-coins to unlock exclusive content and rewards, all secured through blockchain technology.
- **Cross-Platform Support**: Enjoy KizunaCore on Android, iOS, Windows, Mac, and even as a web app.

---

## Key Features

### 1. **Interactive Chat**
Engage in heartfelt conversations with your virtual companion. Powered by AI, the companion learns about you over time, creating a truly personalized experience.

### 2. **Virtual Gifts**
Express your feelings by gifting virtual items to your companion. Unlock new animations, expressions, and interactions by sending thoughtful presents.

### 3. **Blockchain-Powered Economy**
Connect your wallet to buy meme-coins, which can be used to purchase virtual currency and gifts. Blockchain ensures secure and transparent transactions.

### 4. **Live Animations**
Experience lifelike animations of your virtual companion. (Currently using placeholder videos; future updates will include advanced Live2D animations.)

### 5. **Live Wallpaper Mode**
Use your virtual companion as a live wallpaper or lockscreen animation, bringing her into your daily life.
